\textit{"Untuk keluarga, kawan, dan alam"} \newline
"For the sky and the ocean, to respect the Earth and know its limit" \newline

I closed this one-year program with the realization that my study here has somewhat turned to a love letter to my country. It is clear now that my greatest joy is to learn about our Earth, but there is always a special rush within me to explore topics in Indonesia. By this, I am grateful for each instructor in this program who has equipped me with new knowledge and made a space for me to explore topics I care about in classes. I give my special thanks to Erika and Nice who supervised me for this final project. Their class was my first exposure to attribution studies, my current main interest, and I am excited to see the evolving research of this field.

I owe my success in this program to my friends here, fellow diploma students. We spent hours preparing for exams together, practicing presentations and discussing problems. Even in our daily life together--cooking, watching movies, and traveling--we slipped in class topics with humor. It is remarkable to find understanding and camaraderie in each other despite our diverse backgrounds. I am also thankful to have peers who help me welcome feelings by studying our field. It can be sadness, then it can turn to anger and hope as our fuel to fight in our own capabilities. My heart is full because of them and I am forever grateful for the chance to study with them.

Another thing I learned here is to be humbled by our limited time in this world. I wrote this tenderly in honor of Karim who changed my worldview on learning Earth science just from our short interactions, my grandmother who planted my love to nature as a child, and lastly my father who had always been the quite supporter in my life. I like to think they would be pleased that I spent time studying a place they once visited or lived in, and remembered fondly. \newline
