\textit{"Untuk keluarga, kawan, dan alam"} \newline
"For the sky and the ocean, to respect the Earth and know its limit" \newline

I closed this one-year program with the realization that my study here has somewhat turned to a love letter to my country. It is clear now that my greatest joy is to learn about our Earth, but there is always a special rush within me to explore topics in Indonesia. By this, I am grateful for each instructor in this program who has equipped me with new knowledge and made a space for me to explore topics I care about in classes. I give my special thanks to Erika and Nice who supervised me for this final project. Their class was my first exposure to attribution studies, a topic I currently care about the most, and I am excited to see the evolving research of this field.

I owe my success in this program to my friends here, fellow diploma students. We spent hours preparing for exams together, practicing presentations and discussing problems. Even within our daily life together--cooking, watching movies, and travelling--we slip in topics from classes with humor. It is remarkable to find understanding and camaraderie in them despite our diverse backgrounds. I am also thankful to have peers who help welcome big feelings while studying our field. It can be sadness for some time, then anger and hope as our motivation to fight in our own capabilities. My heart is full because of them and I am forever grateful for the chance to study with them.

Another thing I learned from my time here is to be humbled by our limited duration in this world. I wrote this tenderly in honor of Karim who changed my worldview on studying Earth science just from our short interactions, my grandmother who planted my love to nature as a child, and lastly my father who had always been the quite supporter in my life. \newline

August 13, 2026 \newline
Natha
