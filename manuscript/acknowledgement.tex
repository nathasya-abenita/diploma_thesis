\textit{"Untuk keluarga, kawan, dan alam"} \newline
"For the sky and the ocean, to respect the Earth and know its limit" \newline

I close this one-year program with the realization that my study here has somewhat turned to a love letter to my country. It is clear now that my greatest joy is to keep understanding our Earth, but there is always a special rush within me to explore topics in Indonesia. By this, I am grateful for each instructor in this program who has equipped me with new knowledge and made a space for me to explore topics I care about in classes. I give my special thanks to Erika and Nice who supervised me for this final project. Their class was my first exposure to attribution studies, a topic I currently care about the most, and I am excited to see the evolving research of this field.

I owe my success in this program to my friends here, fellow diploma students. We spent hours preparing for exams together, practicing presentations and discussing problems. Even within our daily life together--cooking, watching movies, and travelling--we cannot help to slip in topics from classes with humor. It is remarkable to find understanding and camaraderie despite our diverse backgrounds. I am also thankful to have peers who show me that it is okay to have big feelings while studying our field: be it sadness now, followed by anger and hope as our motivation to fight in our own capabilities. My heart is full because of them and I am forever grateful for the possibilities to study with them.

One thing I learned from my time here is to be humbled by our limited time in this world. I write this in honor of Prof. Karim who changed my worldview in studying Earth science just from our short interactions; of my grandmother who planted my love to nature as a child; of my father who had always been my quite supporter in life. \newline

August 13, 2025 \newline
Natha
